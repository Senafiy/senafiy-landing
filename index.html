What We're Building

📋

Work Order Management

Break complex maintenance into sequenced tasks across trades, with dependency tracking and real-time progress.

🏢

Facility & Asset Registry

Organise properties, buildings, and assets in a structured registry with role-based access for every team member.

💰

Prove the Saving

Every job produces one number: what it cost versus the contractor's quote. Credible, auditable, automatic.

🔧

Direct Vendor Sourcing

Source materials and trades directly. Compare quotes, assign work, and manage payments — no middleman.

📱

Technician Mobile App

Offline-first PWA for technicians on-site. Submit proof of work, update task status, even without connectivity.

🇸🇦

Built for Saudi Arabia

Arabic-first interface, data hosted in Riyadh, aligned with local regulations, and designed around the way Saudi facilities actually operate.
